- Type: boolean

**Admin UI — Cohort Edit Form:** Add a toggle switch in the "Edit cohort" dialog, below the "Open levels" toggles. Label: "Enable workbook download". When on (default), the "Download Workbook" link appears in the sidebar for participants in this cohort. When off, the link is hidden.

**Frontend:** The sidebar reads workbook_enabled from the cohort data. If true, render the Download Workbook link (item 6). If false, do not render it.
